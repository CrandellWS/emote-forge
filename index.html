EMOTE FORGE

Drop a JPEG, PNG or GIF and get it back as an emote that fits each platform's rules. Everything happens in your browser; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Drop an image here or click to choose  ·  you can also paste from the clipboard JPEG · PNG · GIF (animated GIFs stay animated where the platform allows)
Presets: MyPrize 112×112 WebP, under 39 KB, animation kept as animated WebP · Kick 500×500 PNG or GIF, under 1 MB · Discord 128×128 PNG or GIF, under 256 KB. Animated GIF decoding uses the browser's built-in decoder (Chrome and Edge); elsewhere the first frame is used. GIF output is encoded with gif.js (MIT). Source: CrandellWS/emote-forge.
